About Us

Paws & Hearts Animal Rescue was created to help animals who have been abandoned, neglected, or injured. Our mission is to provide these animals with the care and protection they deserve.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue animals in need, provide quality veterinary and shelter services, and connect animals with responsible and loving families.

What We Do

  • Rescue abandoned and neglected animals.
  • Provide temporary shelter and care.
  • Arrange veterinary treatment for injured and sick animals.
  • Find suitable permanent homes for rescued animals.
  • Educate the community about responsible pet ownership.
  • Promote animal welfare and humane treatment.

Our Vision

We envision a community where every animal is treated with kindness, compassion, and respect, and where no healthy, adoptable animal is left without a loving home.

Responsible Pet Ownership

Owning a pet is a rewarding responsibility. Every pet deserves proper care, attention, protection, and love throughout its life.

Responsible Pet Owners Should:

  • Provide clean water and nutritious food.
  • Provide appropriate shelter and a safe environment.
  • Take pets for regular veterinary check-ups.
  • Keep vaccinations up to date.
  • Provide exercise and mental stimulation.
  • Use identification such as a collar or microchip.
  • Spay or neuter pets where appropriate.
  • Never abandon or neglect an animal.

Remember: A pet is a lifelong commitment, not a temporary responsibility.
