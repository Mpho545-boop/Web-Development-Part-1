Fundraising & Community Events

Our events help raise funds for rescued animals while bringing animal lovers and members of the community together.

Annual Paws Walk

Date: 15 September

Location: Community Park

Bring your family and four-legged friends for a fun community walk to raise funds for animal rescue.

Pet Adoption Day

Date: 5 October

Location: Paws & Hearts Rescue Centre

Meet some of our adoptable animals and learn more about giving a rescued pet a permanent home.

Charity Market

Date: 20 October

Location: Local Community Hall

Enjoy food, crafts, games, and family activities while supporting animals in need.
