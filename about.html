About Us

Paws & Hearts Animal Rescue was created to help animals who have been abandoned, neglected, or injured. Our mission is to provide these animals with the care and protection they deserve.

Our Mission

Our mission is to rescue animals in need, provide quality veterinary and shelter services, and connect animals with responsible and loving families.

What We Do

  • Rescue abandoned and neglected animals.
  • Provide temporary shelter and care.
  • Arrange veterinary treatment.
  • Find suitable permanent homes.
  • Educate the community about responsible pet ownership.
  • Promote animal welfare.

Our Vision

We envision a community where every animal is treated with kindness, compassion, and respect.
